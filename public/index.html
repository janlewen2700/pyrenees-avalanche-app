Field Safety Checklist (3x3 Filter)

1. Red Flag Warnings

  • Recent natural or human-triggered avalanches
  • Shooting cracks propagation under skis
  • Hollow "whumpfing" sounds in the snowpack
  • Rapid air temperature increase or heavy rain
  • Recent wind transport creating fresh wind slabs

2. Essential Companion Equipment

  • Transceiver: 3-antenna, worn on body with >80% battery
  • Probe: Minimum 240cm aluminum/carbon probe
  • Shovel: Metal blade (avoid plastic blades)
  • Airbag Backpack: Recommended for slab terrain
